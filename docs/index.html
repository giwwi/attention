A note from the maker

“An article can be good and still not be what you need right now. That’s the idea I’m building Attention around.”

Aleksey Voloschuk — creator of Attention

Attention grew out of the ideas I explore in my book, After Labor and Capital: The Political Economy of the Transitional Period (OSF). It brings that work on the value of information into everyday reading: what is this information worth to you, right now?

Add Attention to Chrome ↗

Free · No Attention account · Bring a profile to get started.

Start with an assistant you already use.

Install Attention, open it, and choose your assistant. Follow the prompt, bring the reply back and review your profile before saving it.

You don’t need an API key to import a profile or use local evaluation.

Latest build: 0.30.2 · Manual installation

The film shows 0.30.2. Store updates follow a separate review schedule.

Download v0.30.2 (ZIP), unzip it, then open chrome://extensions. Switch on Developer mode, choose Load unpacked and select the attention-extension folder.

Updating a manual installation? Replace files in your existing extension folder and click Reload. A separate store installation does not transfer your local profile.
