04 / Your space[ a. ]

Your reading life.
Kept close.

Ordinary evaluation happens in your browser. Your saved profile, reading memory and feedback stay in a local vault, encrypted with a password you choose. No Attention account. No built-in analytics.

External AI is optional and needs your own API key. When you request AI analysis or use a connected service, relevant data goes to that provider. You can lock or erase your local vault.

A note from the maker

“An article can be good and still not be what you need right now. That’s the idea I’m building Attention around.”

Aleksey Voloschuk — creator of Attention

Attention grew out of the ideas I explore in my book, After Labor and Capital: The Political Economy of the Transitional Period (OSF). It brings that work on the value of information into everyday reading: what is this information worth to you, right now?

This is an early version, shaped by real reading and honest feedback. If a recommendation misses the mark, I’d like to hear about it.

Download for Chrome ↓

Free prototype · v0.27.0 · Manual installation

Your context comes first.

  1. 01
    Download & unzip.

    Save the extension above and extract the ZIP.

  2. 02
    Add it to Chrome.

    Open chrome://extensions, switch on Developer mode, then choose Load unpacked and select the extracted folder.

  3. 03
    Start with ChatGPT or Claude.

    Open Attention and create your local vault. Ask a chat that already knows you to prepare a profile, then review and save it. Start with ChatGPT or Claude; you can add the other later.

  4. 04
    Now bring an article.

    Before setup, cards invite you to create your profile. Recommendations start after you save it. Hover over a link in a feed or the title of an open article for your recommendation.

Updating? Replace the files in your existing extension folder, then reload Attention at chrome://extensions. Your saved profile stays in place.

At your keyboard: Tab to the Attention button beside an article title, then Enter to open. Escape to close.
