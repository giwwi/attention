How it works

Not another summary.
A decision for this moment.

1

Set your context

Open an article and click Attention. Create your local vault, then choose work, learning, exploration, or relaxation. Add a topic or skip it. Your first assessment runs locally.

2

See the recommendation

See the current article's score and choose Read, Skim, Save, or Skip. Adjust your scenario or 5, 15, or 30 minute budget to update the recommendation.

3

Make it personal

Tell Attention whether the material was worth it. Future estimates calibrate locally from your feedback.

Local-first by design

Your reading history should not become someone else’s dataset.

Attention works without an account, analytics, or an API key. Local evaluation runs in your browser. Your saved profile, goals, reading history, feedback, and source indexes are protected by a password-encrypted local vault.

Optional AI analysis and connected services receive data when you use those features, as described in the privacy policy. You can lock or erase your local vault. A forgotten password can only be reset by deleting the vault and starting over.

Try the prototype

Install in about a minute.

  1. 01 Download and unzip the extension.
  2. 02 Open chrome://extensions and enable Developer mode.
  3. 03 Click Load unpacked and select the unzipped folder.
  4. 04 Pin Attention, open an article, click its icon, create your vault, and choose your scenario to see the assessment.

Reopen the result with the extension icon, or Tab to the Attention button beside an article title and press Enter. Escape closes the article card.
